In-Context Meaning

Core Definition

Present, appearing, or found everywhere at the same time; omnipresent.

Etymology & Root Evolution

ROOT: Latin 'ubique' meaning 'everywhere'
EVOLUTION: Coined in the mid-19th century from 'ubiquity', originally used in theological context describing omnipresence.
